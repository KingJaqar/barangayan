***CHAPTER 3       	Research Methodology or Operational Framework*** (How will you carry out the research process? What will be your research design? How do you intend to gather the data? What is your unit of analysis? Who are your subjects? What is the sample size? How will you organize, process and analyze your data?)

           	           	In technical developmental research,

* Project Design (project features)  
* Project Development (the steps/phases of development of the project)  
* Testing and Operating Procedure (including validation of strategy employed)  
* Project Evaluation (by projected end-users, experts, etc.  
* Work Plan (Work Breakdown Structure)

IMPORTANT: On all parts of the methodology/framework, integrate Computing Standards and Modern Tools and Techniques that will be used

* *Potential for Commercialization (Discuss the potential of the project to be deployed as a commercially viable system/application)*  
  * *Market Model (Indicate Total Available Market, Servable Available Market, and your Share of Market)*  
  * *Measurable Benefits (What makes the project unique? What benefits will it give to buyers/clients/users?)*  
  * *Three-year Product Roadmap (Discuss the three-year positioning of the project in terms of performance, price and key features)*  
  * *Go-to-Market Strategy (Discuss distribution channels, marketing programs for implementation, marketing plans, and other marketing concerns)*

*Business Model (Discuss where the project will fit in the Value Chain; Revenue Generation Model; Cost Model; Expense Model; Also discuss Success Metrics, Risk Factors and its Mitigation, Revenue Level for Breakeven, etc.)*
