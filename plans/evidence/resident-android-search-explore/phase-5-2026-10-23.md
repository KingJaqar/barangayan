| Android Back: keyboard, then Explore → entry (query kept) → origin | Partly done. | Focus race | **Defect found by the session harness.** The entry's autofocus frame could fire after Explore committed but before the effect cleanup ran. With focus-to-edit in place, that bounced the resident back to the entry | The autofocus frame checks a render-time ref that says whether the entry is still showing |
| Map-origin idle collapse cannot collapse Explore | Done (the idle timer runs only on the entry) | Unchanged; verified natively |

Decision: the return is coordinated from Explore through its own tab navigation's `state` event. I chose this over two alternatives:

- Resetting the target stack to [detail]: it would discard the resident's place in that tab, such as a half-filled request form.
- Moving the result screens under Explore: that is a navigator refactor the plan rules out.
