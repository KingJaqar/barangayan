## Source keys and common rules

Paths below are relative to `apps/resident-web/`. A handler name after a key identifies the terminal presentation owner; it is not a new function to create. The action's account plus entity and deliberate activation are its required notification identity. **Existing** guards/IDs are called out; otherwise an attempt ID is a future wiring requirement, not present coverage. No callback gets a new ID merely because it fired again. Every result must be suppressed after its captured account/entity changes. Errors may persist inline after their single toast; only the touched duplicate live announcement may be adjusted. No global removal of alert/status roles.
