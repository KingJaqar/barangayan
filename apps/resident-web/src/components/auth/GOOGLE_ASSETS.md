Google Sans Medium is bundled for the Google authentication button. The font is
provided by Google Fonts under google-sans-OFL.txt.

Source: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Google+Sans:wght@500
The bundled subset contains characters for the English sign-in, sign-up,
continue and account-linking labels. Request a matching subset when localizing
these labels.

The Google G image in public/google-g.png comes from
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png.
Button design reference: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines.
